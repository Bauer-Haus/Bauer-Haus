Up and Running in a Minute

WindowSmith arranges other apps’ windows, so macOS asks your permission first. This is the whole setup.

1

Install

Open the download and drag WindowSmith into your Applications folder. Launch it and its icon appears in the menu bar — there is no Dock icon and no window.

2

Grant Accessibility

Click Grant Access, then switch WindowSmith on under Privacy & Security › Accessibility. The app unlocks itself the moment you do — no restart, no relaunch.

3

Start Snapping

Pick a layout from the menu bar, or stay on the keyboard: ⌃⌥1–4 for your saved layouts and ⌃⌥←→ to throw a window to the next display.

New versions install themselves — WindowSmith checks for updates and asks before installing one.
